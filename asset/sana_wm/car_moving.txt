Slatted afternoon sunlight streams through the window blinds, casting warm bands of light over splintered floorboards, glassy marbles, scattered toy blocks, and dusty furniture. Subtle motion blur trails the moving car, dust motes drift through the sunbeams, and the camera follows fluidly throughout, maintaining a smooth continuous tracking shot that holds the car at the center of the frame.
